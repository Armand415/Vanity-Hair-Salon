Our Story

A salon built on craft & community

Founded in 2009 in Lakewood, California, Vanity Hair was born from a simple vision: create a salon where clients feel genuinely cared for and stylists love coming to work.

We welcome men, women, and children of all hair types — whether your style is edgy or classic, your hair straight or curly, short or long. Our team of professionally trained stylists brings decades of combined experience to every chair.

We invest continuously in our craft, staying current with the latest techniques in hair and skincare. From precision cuts to extensions, color to waxing, we handle all your beauty needs under one roof.

With over 125 years of combined experience and a genuine aim to please, Vanity Hair is your new home for hair care in Lakewood.
